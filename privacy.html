Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Introduction

HXTMESOC ("we", "our", "the Platform") is a SaaS analytics platform that helps content creators, marketing agencies, and e-commerce brands analyze TikTok content performance. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data.

2. Information We Collect

When users connect their TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Login Kit, we access:

  • Profile Information: Username, display name, avatar, bio, follower/following counts
  • Video Metadata: Public video list including titles, view counts, likes, comments, shares, posting dates
  • Performance Metrics: Aggregated analytics derived from video data
  • Account Email: For subscription management and report delivery

We do NOT collect:

  • TikTok passwords (authentication via OAuth 2.0 only)
  • Private or draft videos
  • Direct messages
  • Payment information from TikTok
  • Data from non-authorized accounts

3. How We Use Information

Data is used to provide our analytics services:

  • Generate performance dashboards and trend reports
  • Provide content optimization recommendations
  • Enable competitor benchmarking (public data only)
  • Deliver scheduled reports via email (Pro/Agency plans)
  • Improve our analytics algorithms with aggregated, anonymized data

We do NOT sell individual user data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Free tier: Data fetched in real-time, 7-day aggregated metrics cached
  • Pro tier: 90-day historical analytics stored
  • Agency tier: 1-year historical analytics stored

All stored data is aggregated performance metrics, not raw personal data. Data is deleted within 30 days of account closure.

5. Data Security

  • All communications encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • OAuth tokens stored encrypted at rest
  • Access controls limit data visibility to authorized account users
  • Regular security audits and compliance reviews

6. TikTok Login and Authorization

HXTMESOC uses TikTok's official Login Kit (OAuth 2.0). Users are redirected to TikTok's login page. We receive an access token, never the password. Users can revoke access in TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. Multi-Account & Agency Use

Agency users may connect multiple TikTok accounts with proper authorization from each account owner. Each connected account must independently authorize access through TikTok's OAuth flow.

8. Third-Party Services

We integrate with:

  • TikTok's official API (content data)
  • Email service provider (report delivery)
  • Payment processor (subscription billing — Pro/Agency only)

9. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. User Rights

  • Access: View all data we hold about your account in your dashboard
  • Export: Download your analytics data in CSV/PDF format
  • Revoke: Disconnect TikTok accounts at any time
  • Delete: Request full account and data deletion

11. Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  1. Log in to HXTMESOC and go to Settings → Delete Account
  2. Revoke access in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
  3. All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

12. Changes to This Policy

We notify users of material changes via email 14 days before they take effect.
