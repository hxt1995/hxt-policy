Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

1. Introduction

HXTMESOC ("we", "our", "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our TikTok content analytics application.

2. Information We Collect

When you log in with your TikTok account, we access the following information through TikTok's official API:

  • Profile Information: Your TikTok username, display name, profile picture, bio, follower count, and following count
  • Video Data: Your public video list including video titles, descriptions, view counts, like counts, comment counts, share counts, and posting dates
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated performance metrics derived from the above data

We do NOT collect:

  • Your TikTok password
  • Your private or draft videos
  • Your direct messages
  • Your payment or financial information
  • Data from other users who have not authorized the App

3. How We Use Information

Data accessed through TikTok's API is used solely for:

  • Displaying your content performance analytics
  • Generating insights about your video performance trends
  • Helping you optimize your content posting strategy

We do NOT sell, share, rent, or distribute any of your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Retention

We do NOT permanently store your TikTok data on our servers. All data is:

  • Fetched in real-time from TikTok's API when you use the App
  • Processed temporarily in memory for analytics calculations
  • Not retained after your session ends

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data during processing:

  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely and never exposed
  • We follow TikTok's API security best practices

6. TikTok Login and Authorization

HXTMESOC uses TikTok's official Login Kit for authentication. When you log in:

  • You are redirected to TikTok's official login page
  • You authorize specific permissions for our App
  • TikTok sends us an access token (not your password)
  • You can revoke access anytime in TikTok Settings → Security → "Manage app permissions"

7. Third-Party Services

This App integrates exclusively with TikTok's official API. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View what data we access from your TikTok account
  • Revoke: Remove our App's access to your TikTok data at any time through TikTok's settings
  • Delete: Request deletion of any temporarily cached data by contacting us
  • Opt-out: Stop using the App at any time

10. Data Deletion

Since we do not permanently store your data, there is nothing to delete. However, if you want to ensure complete disconnection:

  1. Go to TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
  2. Find HXTMESOC and revoke access
  3. Contact us at trunghx363@gmail.com to confirm deletion

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
